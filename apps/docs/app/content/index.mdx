# SkyScribe SDK

The SkyScribe SDK lets you show content written in [SkyScribe](https://skyscribe.app) on a website you build yourself: an **SDK-driven website**. Authors write and publish in SkyScribe, the content lives in their own Bluesky account, and the SDK fetches it for your site.

<Callout type="tip" title="Not a developer?">
  You probably want a SkyScribe Site instead: pick a design, choose colours and fonts, and launch, with no coding. See [SkyScribe Sites vs. SDK-driven websites](https://skyscribe.app/users-guide/getting-started/skyscribe-sites-vs-your-own-frontend).
</Callout>
